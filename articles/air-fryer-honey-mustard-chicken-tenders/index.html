Air Fryer Honey Mustard Chicken Tenders

October 19, 2026

Finding a dinner that the whole family loves can sometimes feel like a challenge, but these Air Fryer Honey Mustard Chicken Tenders are a guaranteed winner. They perfectly balance the sweet notes of honey with the sharp tang of Dijon mustard, all wrapped in a satisfyingly crunchy coating. This recipe takes a childhood classic and gives it a sophisticated, flavor-packed twist.
