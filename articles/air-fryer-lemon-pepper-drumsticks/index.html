Air Fryer Lemon Pepper Drumsticks

October 19, 2026

These Air Fryer Lemon Pepper Drumsticks are a game-changer for weeknight dinners. They combine the zesty, bright flavor of lemon with the bold kick of cracked black pepper, all while achieving a perfectly crispy skin that rivals deep-fried chicken. Using an air fryer means you get that satisfying crunch with significantly less oil, making this a healthier alternative that the whole family will love.
