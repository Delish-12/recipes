Crispy Air Fryer Chicken Fajitas

October 19, 2026

Imagine sizzling fajitas without the grease or the heavy cleanup of a stovetop skillet. Air fryer chicken fajitas are a total game-changer for busy weeknights when you want a healthy, restaurant-quality meal in a flash. The air fryer circulates hot air to create those perfectly charred edges on the peppers and onions while keeping the chicken incredibly juicy and tender.

Servings: 4
