Air Fryer Sausage and Peppers

October 19, 2026

Air Fryer Sausage and Peppers is the ultimate weeknight dinner solution for anyone craving a hearty, flavorful meal without the hassle of standing over a hot stove. This classic Italian-American dish comes together beautifully in the air fryer, resulting in perfectly browned sausages and tender-crisp vegetables in a fraction of the time it takes in a traditional oven.
