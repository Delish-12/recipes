Air Fryer Halloween Pumpkin Pop Tarts

October 19, 2026

Halloween is the perfect time for spooky treats, and these Air Fryer Halloween Pumpkin Pop Tarts are about to become your new favorite fall tradition. Forget the store-bought toaster pastries; these homemade versions are flaky, filled with warm spiced pumpkin, and so much fun to decorate with the kids. Using the air fryer ensures a perfectly golden crust in a fraction of the time it takes to preheat a traditional oven.
