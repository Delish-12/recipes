Air Fryer Avocado Toast with Egg

October 19, 2026

Starting your morning with a nutritious and satisfying breakfast has never been easier thanks to the air fryer. This air fryer avocado toast with egg combines the creamy texture of ripe avocado with a perfectly cooked egg, all nestled on a piece of crispy, golden-brown bread. It is a quick meal that feels like a gourmet brunch right in your own kitchen.
