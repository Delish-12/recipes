Air Fryer Sausage Egg and Cheese Biscuits

October 19, 2026

Imagine waking up to the smell of golden, buttery biscuits and savory breakfast sausage without having to stand over a hot stove for an hour. Air Fryer Sausage Egg and Cheese Biscuits are the ultimate morning game-changer. By using the air fryer, you get that perfect crispy exterior on the biscuit while the cheese melts into every nook and cranny. This recipe is designed for busy mornings when you need a hearty, protein-packed meal but only have a few minutes to spare.
