Crispy Air Fryer Mozzarella Sticks

October 19, 2026

There is nothing quite like the satisfaction of a perfectly melted, gooey mozzarella stick with a crunch that can be heard across the room. While these are usually reserved for restaurant appetizers or frozen snacks, the air fryer has changed the game. You can now achieve that golden-brown crust and cheese-pull perfection right in your own kitchen without the hassle and calories of deep frying.

Recipe Information

Prep Time: 15 minutes (plus 1 hour freezing time)

Cook Time: 6 minutes

Total Time: 1 hour 21 minutes

Servings: 6 servings (2 sticks per serving)

Difficulty Level: Easy

Nutrition Information

Calories per serving: 185 kcal

Protein: 12g

Carbohydrates: 14g

Fat: 9g

Fiber: 1g

Sugar: 1g

Sodium: 480mg
