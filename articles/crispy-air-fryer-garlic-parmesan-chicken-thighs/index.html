Crispy Air Fryer Garlic Parmesan Chicken Thighs

October 19, 2026

There is something truly magical about the combination of garlic and parmesan cheese, especially when paired with juicy chicken thighs. Using an air fryer takes this classic flavor profile to a whole new level by creating an incredibly crispy exterior while keeping the meat tender and moist inside. This recipe is perfect for busy weeknights when you want a gourmet-tasting meal without the fuss of deep frying or long oven times.
