Air Fryer Pumpkin Spice Donuts

October 19, 2026

When the crisp autumn air arrives, there is nothing quite like the smell of cinnamon and pumpkin wafting through the kitchen. These Air Fryer Pumpkin Spice Donuts are the ultimate fall treat, offering all the cozy flavors of a traditional bakery donut without the mess and hassle of deep-frying. They are soft, cakey, and coated in a crunchy cinnamon-sugar layer that makes every bite feel like a celebration of the season.

Cook Time: 10 minutes

Total Time: 25 minutes

Servings: 12 donuts

Difficulty Level: Easy
