Air Fryer Pecan Pie Bites

October 19, 2026

Indulging in a classic Southern dessert has never been easier than with these Air Fryer Pecan Pie Bites. Traditional pecan pie is a holiday staple, but making a full-sized pie can be time-consuming and often leaves you with way too many leftovers. These bite-sized treats offer everything you love about the original—the flaky crust, the gooey caramel-like center, and the crunch of toasted pecans—in a fraction of the time.

Recipe Information

Prep Time: 15 minutes

Cook Time: 10 minutes

Total Time: 25 minutes

Servings: 12 bites

Difficulty Level: Easy
